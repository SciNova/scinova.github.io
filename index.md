### Free-Software Libraries

SciNova maintains a comprehensive stack of open‑source libraries that span pure mathematics, astronomical calculation, geolocation, sensor fusion, and Hebrew‑specific text and calendar processing. All are released under the GNU General Public License v3.

#### Core Libraries

* **Algebra** – 2D/3D vectors, quaternions, and matrix operations for sensor fusion and 3D math.
* **Date** – Gregorian calendar arithmetic (Date, DateTime, Time), spans, and week‑day calculations.
* **Astro** – Solar and lunar position, phase, rise/set times, and shared astronomical constants.
* **Geo** – Earth models, coordinate distances, bearing, and a global place database with diaspora detection.
* **Sensors** – Gyroscope, accelerometer, magnetometer, barometer, GPS, and a full Kalman‑filter sensor‑fusion engine.

#### Hebrew Libraries (Reuben Institute)

* **Hebrew** – Unicode‑aware Hebrew text utilities, punctuation handling, and diacritic stripping.
* **HebrewNumbers** – Gematria conversion and formatting.
* **HebrewDate** – Full Hebrew calendar (conversion, holidays, Torah readings) with locale‑aware month names.

These Hebrew libraries were developed at the **Reuben Institute** using the SciNova core libraries and are released under the same GPLv3 terms.
